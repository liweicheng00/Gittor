A new Worktrees tab with a search and favorites, and a Stale group for branches that nobody works on.

## New
- The Worktrees tab lists the worktrees of the repository. Search them by name, folder or branch, and star your favorites to keep them at the top. Press Ctrl+6 to open it.
- The Branches tab has a Stale group. It shows the local branches without a commit for 30 days, and the branches whose tracked branch is gone from the remote.
- Right-click a branch and select Mark as Stale or Remove from Stale to choose the stale branches yourself. Settings › General sets the number of days.
- The Review window can start an Interactive Rebase of the commits of the review, or from one of its commits.
- The log marks a tag that the remote does not have. The menu of the commit pushes the tag.

## Improved
- A file of the Files tab opens in the center panel, in a tab next to the log, as in an IDE. You can add notes to its lines.
- The left tabs show larger icons, and their tooltips give their names and shortcuts. Open and Refresh in the toolbar show only their icons, and the toolbar no longer repeats the name of the project.
- The View menu no longer has the Highlight options, which were of little use.
- The Compare window lists the notes of a file, with Copy All and a field for a note on the whole file.
- More buttons tell their shortcuts in their tooltips, and the tooltips follow the changes in Settings › Keymap.
- In the Interactive Rebase editor, the squash arrow points to the commit below.

## Fixed
- In the Plasma and Ember themes, the primary buttons no longer show light lines at their sides.
