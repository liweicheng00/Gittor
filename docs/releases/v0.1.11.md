Gittor fits a narrow window, and Ctrl+F finds text in an open file.

## New
- Gittor fits a narrow window, for example a side panel next to your editor. In a medium width, the commit details show under the log. In a narrow width, all parts show in one column, and More shows the other toolbar buttons.
- Ctrl+F finds text in the file that is open in the center panel, also in the Markdown preview. A find button is in the toolbar of the file.

## Improved
- Ctrl+W closes the open file first. Without an open file, it closes the project tab.
- The tooltips give short names, and keep longer text only where it shows more information.

## Fixed
- The reset buttons of Settings › Keymap show the default keys at once.
- With View › Left Panel off, the log keeps its width, and the setting stays after a restart.
