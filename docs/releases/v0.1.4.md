Switch the project tabs with the keyboard, and filter the log with the branch picker.

## New
- Alt+1 to Alt+8 show the project tab at that place, and Alt+9 shows the last tab. Settings › Keymap can change these keys.

## Improved
- The Branch filter of the log opens the branch picker. It has a search field and the groups Recent, Local and Remote. You can check more than one branch.
- A double click on a local change opens Compare with the files of its changelist only. This also applies to .ignore and to the unversioned files.
