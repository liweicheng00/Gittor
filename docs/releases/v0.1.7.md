Two new glass themes, Plasma and Ember, with a soft glow of color behind clear text.

## New
- The themes Plasma and Ember give Gittor a glass look. A soft light of color shows through the panels, and all text stays clear.
- Plasma uses cyan, blue, violet and magenta. Ember uses gold, orange, red and magenta.
- Select a theme with the Theme button in the toolbar, or in Settings › General.
- In these themes, the diff, the commit graph and the menus get matching colors. The fields with the focus glow.
