Gittor

Git, the IntelliJ way. Without the IDE.

The log, the merge window and local reviews, each in its own window. A light app without a JVM.

The 90-second tour

The log, recent projects, changelists, a squash with Undo, conflicts, a local review and the small tools.

The IntelliJ log

The same graph layout, IntelliSort and branch colors. Large repositories scroll without a commit limit.

Safe history edits

Squash, drop, reword and fixups run in memory. Almost every operation has Undo.

Changelists

Group local changes, move single hunks between changelists, and commit only the checked changes.

Conflicts and reviews

A three-way merge window, and local reviews of a branch with notes, without a remote.
